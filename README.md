## 🌟 Core Features & Modules

### 1. 📱 Parent Phone & WhatsApp Login
- Parents can log in directly using their Ghanaian mobile phone numbers without needing an email address.

### 2. 💬 1-Click WhatsApp Direct Reminders & Broadcast Hub
- One-click personalized WhatsApp fee reminder links (`https://wa.me/...`) pre-filled with student fee arrears (`GH₵ 1,450.00`), MTN MoMo merchant numbers, and term deadlines.
- Mass announcement broadcast center for exam schedules and PTA circulars.

### 3. 📊 Standards-Based Ghanaian Academics (NaCCA & GES)
- Automated Continuous Assessment (50% SBA: Class Tests, Projects, Homework) + 50% End of Term Examination.
- Automatic Stanine 1–9 grade scale and descriptive mastery ratings.

### 4. 🖨️ Printable Official Documents
- **Ghanaian Terminal Report Cards** with Stanine rankings and teacher remarks.
- **Fee Payment Receipts (`LIS-REC-XXXX`)** with double-entry audit trails.
- **Staff Monthly Payslips** with SSNIT (5.5% / 13.5%) & GRA PAYE withholding tax.

### 5. 🛡️ Anonymous Whistleblower Case Tracker
- Secret ticket PIN tracking (`LIS-SAFE-XXXX`) for reporting bullying, safety, and staff welfare with zero personal identity logging.

### 6. 📁 1-Click Data Exports (CSV / Excel)
- Pupil Registry, Debtors Aging Matrix, Staff Payroll Remittance, and BECE Registration Candidates.
